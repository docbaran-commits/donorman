DonorMan

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · App ID com.docbaran.donorman

Summary

DonorMan is a clinical calculator. It does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. Calculations run on your device. There is no account, no login, and no analytics.

Information the app does not collect

DonorMan does not collect:

  • Names, contact details, or identifiers
  • Health records or patient identifiers
  • Location, photos, contacts, or microphone/camera data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising IDs
  • Payment or purchase information (the app is free and has no in-app purchases)

Information you enter

You may enter sex, age, height, and weight for a recipient and a donor so the app can compute predicted heart mass (PHM), BMI, and body surface area.

Those values:

  • Stay on your device for the current session
  • Are not uploaded to a server
  • Are not saved to iCloud, HealthKit, or any cloud account by DonorMan
  • Are discarded when you leave or quit the app (the app does not persist them)

Do not enter names, medical record numbers, or other identifiers. The calculator does not need them.

Network use

PHM, BMI, and BSA are computed locally. DonorMan does not require a network connection to calculate results.

The optional web / PWA version may load static assets from the hosting site when you open it in a browser. That hosting provider may see standard web server logs (for example IP address and user agent), which DonorMan does not control or combine with calculator inputs.

Third parties

DonorMan does not integrate advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social login, or crash-reporting services.

Children

DonorMan is intended for clinicians and other adults involved in transplant sizing. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect children’s data.

Medical disclaimer

DonorMan is a calculation aid for adult heart-transplant size matching. It is not a medical device diagnosis tool, not a substitute for clinical judgment or institutional protocols, and not validated for pediatric recipients.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted at this URL, with a revised effective date.
